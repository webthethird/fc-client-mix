Reading this honestly

The series starts when tracking started, not because anything happened that day. Nothing here shows a before-and-after for the October vote — the client dimension does not exist for earlier dates and does not backfill.

Single days swing hard. Deletions were 12,805 one day and 1,813 the next. One automated account moves message share by tens of percent; it barely moves account share.

“self” is not one app. It means the signing key was registered to the author’s own account rather than to an app — the pattern Quilcast uses, among others. It is reliably not the official client, and not reliably any single alternative.

This measures one node’s view. Hypersnap and Snapchain currently share validators, so today that is the whole network. After the announced fork it will not be.
